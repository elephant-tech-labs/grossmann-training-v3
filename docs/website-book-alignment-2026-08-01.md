# Website Book Alignment

## Source of truth

The website content and information architecture are aligned to these client documents:

- `Webseitenbuch_GT_V12_Master_mit_Fallbeispielen.docx`
- `Stimmen ueber Grossmann Training - Top 10 mit Fotos fuer die Webseite V3.docx`

When future copy conflicts with older drafts, the Master Document and the Top 10 testimonials document take precedence.

## Information architecture

The primary decision path is:

1. Fuer wen? - Zielgruppen
2. Wobei? - Themen
3. Wie? - Veranstaltungsformen
4. Beweis - Praxisbeispiele, Referenzen und Stimmen
5. Wer? - Bernd Grossmann, Qualifikation und Grossmann Training & Kollegen
6. Kontakt

This hierarchy is exposed in the persistent three-line menu. Desktop quick links are secondary shortcuts, not a replacement for the full structure.

## Content rules

- Use the Master Document wording directly wherever it defines positioning, target groups, formats, cases, qualifications, or FAQs.
- Preserve the five case-study structure: Ausgangssituation, Herausforderung, Vorgehen, Wirkung, Bildunterschrift, Kontaktimpuls.
- Keep proof before detailed explanation where possible.
- Distinguish quotations from editorial context. In particular, only `Ich bin ... sprachlos!` is the direct Stefan Zoeller quote; the subsequent commissions are context.
- The YouTube speaker is a Service Manager, client, and commissioning decision-maker. He is not a seminar participant.
- Where a testimonial has no approved portrait, use initials rather than an unrelated person.
- The references page contains all ten selected testimonials in the supplied order and context.

## Brand and photography

- Official logo files live under `public/images/brand/`.
- Curated client photography lives under `public/images/grossmann-v4/`.
- Bernd appears prominently in the homepage hero and on the About and Contact pages.
- Page photography must be chosen by visible subject and context, not filename alone.
- Maintain fixed image frames and explicit `object-position` values so faces remain visible on desktop and mobile.

## Deferred launch items

- Zoho form embed and final field mapping
- Approved legal text for Impressum and Datenschutz
- Optional hidden `Wissen & Praxis` editorial section
- Any additional testimonial portraits or company logos that still require approval
